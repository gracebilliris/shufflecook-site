Common questions

How do I shuffle a recipe?

Open Shuffle and tap Shuffle Again, or shake the device from any tab.

How do I create a shopping list?

Open a recipe, tap Add to Shopping List, then open Shopping.

How do I remove a recipe?

In Shopping, swipe left on a recipe and tap Delete, or use Edit.

Where is my data stored?

Selected recipes and collected-item state remain in ShuffleCook's private on-device storage and are excluded from backups. ShuffleCook has no accounts or cloud sync. See the privacy policy.

Recipe Roulette on the web

Open Recipe Roulette in a modern browser. Choose pantry ingredients to see recipes using at least one of them, then narrow by dietary preference or cooking time. With no pantry selected, all recipes are available to explore.

Choose Surprise me to pick from your current matches, use a heart to save a favourite, or open Let's cook for quantities and full instructions. Add missing gathers ingredients into your shopping list, where you can tick them off. Quantities are shown per recipe, not combined.

Your web pantry, favourites and shopping list are saved only in the current browser. There is no account or sync with the native app. Use Reset saved data to start over. If saving is blocked, the web app displays a warning and remains usable for the current visit. For web support, include your browser version and reproduction steps in the tracker above.
