Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026

No personal data collection

ShuffleCook does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

All 100 recipes and their images are included in the installed application. Selected shopping-list recipes and collected-item state are stored only in the app's private storage on the user's device and are excluded from device backups.

ShuffleCook has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party software development kits, in-app purchases, network client, or backend service.

Deleting the app

Deleting ShuffleCook removes its locally stored shopping-list data. Apple may independently process App Store downloads, purchases, diagnostics, or other information under Apple's privacy policy.

External links and support

Only when a user chooses a privacy-policy or support link does ShuffleCook hand that link to the device's browser to open GitHub. GitHub may process browser and account information under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Support requests submitted through GitHub Issues are public and may be viewed by the developer and other visitors. Do not include personal or sensitive information.

Privacy questions can be submitted through the ShuffleCook support page.
